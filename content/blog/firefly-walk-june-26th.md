Hello friends!

In partnership with the [Pascommuck Conversation Trust](https://www.pctland.org/), we will be hosting a firefly walk on the Trust's Old Trolley Line Conversation Area! We'd love for you to join us!

We'll meet at the intersection of Clapp and the trolley line, 19 Clapp St, Easthampton, MA 01027, at 815 PM on the evening of the 26th. We'll tell you a bit about fireflies, and their intersection with native plants and dark skies and all the rest, and then we'll walk out into the fields and marvel at the majesty of these incredibly cool insects!

Please come prepared! You'll want bug spray, potentially bug netting if that's your thing, and red light (red light only!) headlights or flashlights!   
Please be prepared to leave your cell phones put away and silenced, and please be prepared for a few minutes of silence! Groups can be chatty and part of the firefly experience is definitely the night noises!

If you plan to come, please [RSVP](https://forms.gle/N24JFtmrgpZ6t8556) using this form!

If you have any questions you can let us know via the form!

In other news, we need new members to join the BEES committee! We have a lot of good folks and a lot of great ideas (more about those ideas coming soon), but if you have any interest at all, please reach out to me at sga.politics@proton.me. We have a lot of fun and we're doing some good; a few more people will really help us be able to do a log of good!

We look forward to seeing you soon!
