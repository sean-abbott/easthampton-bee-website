## The BEES Committee

The Easthampton Biodiversity, Environment, and Ecological Sustainability (BEES) Committee was formed to help guide Easthampton on a journey of increasing our climate, environmental, and ecological resilience. We intend to take concrete steps in order to provide the citizens of Easthampton with direct, measurable, and observable actions that they can take that will increase not only the community and environment's resilience, but their own.

You can also find our [committee page on the Easthampton town website](https://easthamptonma.gov/744/Biodiversity-Environment-Ecological-Sust).
