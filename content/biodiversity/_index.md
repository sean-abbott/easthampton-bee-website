We are in the midst of a "polycrisis", meaning there multiple crises happening at the same time. One of those crises is biodiversity: we're losing it. There is good news though: more than any of the problems we face, each individual can take personal action to reverse the loss of biodiversity, and each person will be able to see the results of their own work. It's inspiring and invigorating.
